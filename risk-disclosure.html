1. General Risk Warning

Trading futures, equities, options, forex, and cryptocurrency involves substantial risk of loss and is not suitable for every investor. You could lose part or all of your invested capital, and in the case of leveraged instruments like futures, you may lose more than your original investment. Only funds that you can afford to lose without affecting your standard of living should ever be used for trading or speculation.

Every Briefing, Playbook, and piece of content published by EightyTwentyVentures is provided strictly for educational and informational purposes. None of it is a recommendation to take any specific position, enter any specific trade, or pursue any specific strategy in your own account. 4. The Specific Risks of Futures and Leverage

Futures and other leveraged instruments allow you to control a large notional position with a comparatively small amount of capital. This magnifies both gains and losses. You may be required to deposit additional margin on short notice if a position moves against you, and a position can be liquidated by your broker without your consent if margin requirements are not met. Past price behavior in any market, including behavior described in this Site's content, is not indicative of future results.
