How the Writing Is Structured

The EightyTwenty Lens

Every piece runs through the same intellectual architecture. Not as a checklist, but as a filter: identifying the 20% of inputs that determine 80% of outcomes, then writing only about those.

01

Market Structure

How auctions actually work: where value gets accepted and rejected, how inflection zones form, and what price and volume are saying when read together.

02

Positioning & Regime

Where informed capital is moving before the narrative catches up, what regime the broader market is operating in, and which playbook actually fits current conditions.

03

Execution

The mechanics of getting in and out: entries, risk framed through opposing inventory, trade grading, and managing a position by progress rather than the clock.

04

Expectation Shifts

Markets move on changing future expectations, not on current conditions. The edge is in identifying where the market is about to revise its probability distribution before that revision is consensus.

05

Psychology & Process

Mental capital, discipline, ownership, and the behavioral side of a framework. The part that determines whether the analysis ever actually gets used.

06

Business & Performance

Lessons from operating as a trader and builder: what compounds, what doesn't, and the broader observations that don't fit neatly into a single chart.

Every Briefing applies one or more of these layers to a specific situation in the market. The Playbooks go deeper into each one.

The Archive

What you'll find here

The Briefing is not a scheduled newsletter. It is a growing database, pieces written when a topic warrants it, organized so they can be found when you need them. Some pieces work through market structure and auction theory at the level of a single session. Others map a broader regime or look at where positioning is shifting before the narrative catches up. Others are about execution, psychology, or the business and performance lessons that come from actually doing this. The only constant is the EightyTwenty framework running underneath all of it: the same lens applied to a different situation in the market.
