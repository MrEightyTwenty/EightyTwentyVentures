Chapter 03

Mapping the Macro Regime

Individual equity selection without macro context is like choosing which boat to board without checking whether the harbor is calm or about to experience a storm. Both boats matter. But one of those questions is more important than the other, and the answer to it changes which boats are worth boarding at all.

Why the Regime Comes Before the Stock

The same business that compounds spectacularly in one macro environment can be destroyed by another. A company with extraordinary operational quality, growing revenue, and competent management will still perform poorly if the macro backdrop is structurally hostile to its business model, its cost of capital, or the investor appetite for its sector. Getting the macro regime right does not guarantee good outcomes. Getting it wrong virtually guarantees bad ones, regardless of how well the individual stock selection is executed.

This framework begins with the broadest possible read of the world and progressively narrows it. Economic cycles, geopolitical forces, monetary policy trajectories, and capital flow dynamics establish the backdrop. From that backdrop, the dominant theme for the current environment is identified. From the theme, sectors and industries that are structurally positioned to benefit are selected. From those sectors, the individual equities that represent the highest-quality vehicles for capturing that benefit are identified. The stock is the final decision, not the first one.

Four Variables That Define Any Macro Regime

  • InflationRising, falling, or stable relative to central bank targets? Inflation's trajectory determines which assets are repriced positively or negatively and constrains the policy options available, affecting the real return from different asset classes.
  • GrowthIs the economy expanding or contracting? Is corporate revenue growing or declining? Growth determines earnings trajectory and credit availability, which directly affect what investors are willing to pay for future earnings.
  • Rates and LiquidityWhere are interest rates in the cycle, and which direction is policy moving? Rate levels affect the discount rate applied to future earnings. Liquidity conditions affect willingness to own risk assets.
  • ProductivityIs technology, infrastructure, or organizational innovation improving the economy's output per unit of input? Productivity gains suppress inflation while supporting growth: the most favorable combination for equity ownership.

From Macro to Theme

Once the regime is mapped, the dominant investment theme becomes identifiable. Themes are not forecasts. They are observations about structural change already underway that the market is beginning to price. AI infrastructure buildout driven by data center demand is a theme. Energy transition investment altering the capital expenditure landscape is a theme. Fiscal consolidation redirecting capital from government expansion to private investment is a theme.

The identification of the theme comes before the identification of the sector. Working in reverse, picking sectors and then constructing a macro narrative to justify them, is reverse-engineered analysis that tends to produce positions in the right sectors at the wrong time in the cycle. The theme is the starting point, not the conclusion.

Themes are the translation layer between global forces and individual market opportunities. A geopolitical shift, a policy change, or a technological development becomes actionable when it can be traced through a specific theme to a specific sector to a specific set of businesses that benefit from it materially and measurably.

Branching: From Theme to Opportunity

  • Theme established: identify the macro force and the structural change it is producing in the economy
  • Sector identification: which broad sectors benefit directly and materially from this theme?
  • Industry narrowing: within those sectors, which specific industries are most leveraged to the theme's primary driver?
  • Equity selection: within those industries, which company has the strongest relative strength, the most compelling fundamental trajectory, and the cleanest structural entry point?

Each step of the branching process eliminates candidates based on structural logic rather than surface-level appeal. A company that looks exciting in isolation but does not survive the sector filter is not the right vehicle for the thesis, regardless of its individual quality. The thesis drives the selection. The selection never creates the thesis retroactively.

Geopolitical Context as a Modifier

Geopolitical conditions are not separate from the macro analysis. They are a modifier applied on top of the economic regime. A commodity thesis that is correct economically can be disrupted by trade policy. An industrial reshoring thesis can be accelerated by geopolitical rivalry. Reading the geopolitical layer accurately does not require predicting specific events. It requires understanding which of your existing theses are exposed to geopolitical risk and which are amplified by geopolitical tailwinds.
