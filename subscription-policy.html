Subscription Policy

Last updated: July 17, 2026

1. The Current Model

As of the date above, EightyTwentyVentures is entirely free. There is no paid tier, no premium subscription, and no membership fee for any content published on this Site, including the Briefing archive and the Playbooks. Registering with your email does not create a billing relationship of any kind.

2. What "Subscribing" Means Today

When you enter your email through the newsletter signup or the registration form, you are opting in to receive new content by email and, where applicable, gaining access to registered-only pages on the Site. That is the entire scope of the relationship. No payment method is collected or required at any point in this process.

3. Unsubscribing

You may unsubscribe from email communications at any time using the link included in every email we send. Unsubscribing stops future emails; it does not delete your underlying account or data, which is governed separately by the Privacy Policy.

4. If a Paid Offering Is Introduced

Should EightyTwentyVentures introduce a paid product in the future, such as coaching, a membership tier, or a tool package, it will be presented as a distinct, clearly labeled offering with its own explicit pricing, terms, and opt-in. No free subscriber will be automatically enrolled in or charged for a paid offering without separately and affirmatively agreeing to it. This policy will be updated to reflect the specific terms of any such offering before it launches.

5. Refunds

Because no payment is currently collected for any content or access on this Site, no refund process currently applies. Should a paid offering be introduced, its specific refund terms will be disclosed at that time and reflected in an update to this page.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Subscription Policy as the site's offerings change. Material changes will be reflected by an updated "Last updated" date at the top of this page, and any shift from the current free model will be communicated clearly rather than folded in silently.
