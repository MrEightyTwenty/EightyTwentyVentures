Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 17, 2026

1. What We Collect

We collect the information you provide directly: your email address when you sign up for the newsletter or register for site access. We do not collect payment information, as no paid products are currently offered through this Site.

Standard technical data (such as browser type, general location inferred from IP address, and pages visited) may be collected automatically through the Site's hosting infrastructure for basic operational and security purposes.

2. How We Use It

Your email address is used to send the content you signed up for, including new Briefings and site updates, and to manage your registered access to the Site. We do not sell, rent, or trade your email address or any personal information to third parties.

3. Where Your Data Lives

Site infrastructure, form submissions, and account registration are handled through Netlify's hosting, forms, and identity services. Your data is subject to Netlify's own security practices as our infrastructure provider. We do not maintain a separate database of personal information outside of what these services provide.

4. Cookies and Local Storage

The Site may use minimal cookies or browser local storage strictly to maintain your logged-in session after registration. We do not use third-party advertising trackers or cross-site tracking cookies.

5. Third-Party Services

This Site uses Google Fonts (for typography) and Netlify (for hosting, forms, and authentication). These providers may process limited technical data as part of delivering their service. We do not control their respective privacy practices; refer to their own policies for detail.

6. Your Rights

You may unsubscribe from email communications at any time using the link included in every email. You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data by reaching out through the social channels linked on the Site. We will respond to and honor such requests within a reasonable time.

7. Children's Privacy

This Site is not directed at, and does not knowingly collect information from, anyone under the age of 18. If you believe a minor has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will remove it.

8. International Visitors

This Site is operated from and hosted in the United States. If you access the Site from outside the United States, you understand that your information will be transferred to and processed in the United States, which may have data protection laws different from those of your home jurisdiction.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Material changes will be reflected by an updated "Last updated" date at the top of this page.

10. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy, or requests regarding your data, can be directed through the social channels linked on the Site.
