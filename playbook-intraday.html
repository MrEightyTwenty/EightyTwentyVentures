Short-Term · Intra-Session

The Auction-Market-Theory Playbook

A complete short-term trading system built on one foundational idea: markets are continuous auctions between buyers and sellers. It was not invented from scratch. It was assembled, organized, and adapted from principles that have been part of how markets work since organized exchanges first existed, and from years spent learning alongside other traders and educators. Eight chapters, read in sequence the first time, returned to as reference after that.

Eight Chapters

The complete system.

Read in sequence the first time. Return to individual chapters as reference points. Each one builds on the last.

Chapter 01

First Principles

Why Auction Market Theory, the mentors and educators this system was assembled from, and what "freedom of time" actually means as a design constraint.

 Chapter 02

Establishing Context

Market Generated Information as the primary source of context, why overnight data gets read at a discount, and the four places where the majority of useful context lives.

 Chapter 03

Inflections as Decision Points

How stalled negotiations become zones, why a level is a question rather than an answer, and the higher-timeframe references that bring multiple groups to the table at once.

 Chapter 04

Translating the Tape

Reasoning through what a candle is actually saying, auction-oriented exhaustion and absorption, and how reversal and continuation sequences really unfold.

 Chapter 05

Setting a Bias

Day type identification across four environments, including catalyst-driven sessions, and why each one demands a completely different response.

 Chapter 06

Don't Take the Market for a Liar

The full intraday reference framework: prior session extremes, overnight value structure, opening range and initial balance levels, and the midpoints that tie them together.

 Chapter 07

Implementing Execution & The Bus-Stop Theory

The stages of a trading decision, risk defined through opposing inventory, prime positioning within higher-timeframe structure, pyramiding, sizing, and progress-based trade management.

 Chapter 08

The War Between Your Ears

Ten foundational commandments, mental capital, journaling as audit, and the feedback loop that compounds everything else in this playbook.
